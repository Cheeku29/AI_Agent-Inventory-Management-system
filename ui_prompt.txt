# PHASE 3 — ESTABLISH A DESIGN SYSTEM

Before redesigning individual pages, create one consistent design language.

## Color palette

Use a professional B2B SaaS palette.

Primary:
Deep navy / dark blue

Background:
Very light neutral / warm white

Surface:
White

Border:
Very subtle gray

Text:
Dark neutral

Gray

Warning:

Danger:
Subtle red

Do NOT use:

* excessive purple
* neon colors
* rainbow gradients
* giant gradients
* random colors
* different accent colors on every page
